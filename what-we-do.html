WHAT WE DO

We build back-office software for organisations, focusing on FinTech and PayTech.

Banks and payment businesses often end up with back-office solutions that lack proper configuration and smooth upgrade paths over time. We build software that uses the intelligence available via AI to help solve those problem areas — security-first, test-first, cloud-native and API-only, and built to run in isolation according to each organisation's requirements.

Alongside the software we build, we also give client teams the tooling to configure their own systems and validate them thoroughly before they reach production.

We are actively developing this software today. Details will be published here soon.

WHERE WE FOCUS

FinTech and PayTech, back office first.

We focus on the operational and back-office problems inside financial and payments businesses — the processes where a mistake reaches a real banking customer. Our work is judged by whether it keeps that customer safe, not just whether it works in a demo.
