Cookie & Tracking Policy

Effective date: 7 September 2026  ·  Last updated: 7 September 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website can ask your browser to store, typically to remember information between requests or visits.

2. Similar technologies

"Similar technologies" refers to mechanisms such as local storage, session storage, or server-side logging that can serve a comparable function to cookies.

What this Website actually uses today: This Website does not set first-party marketing or analytics cookies. It does not use browser local storage or session storage for tracking. The technologies below reflect what is actually deployed, not a generic list.

3. Strictly necessary technologies

These are technologies required for the Website to function at a basic level. This Website is a static site and does not currently set application cookies of this kind beyond what its hosting/CDN provider applies at the infrastructure level (see Section 4).

4. Security technologies

The Website is served through a content delivery network (CDN) and security provider (Cloudflare). Cloudflare may set cookies or use similar mechanisms to help protect the Website and its infrastructure — for example, to help distinguish legitimate traffic from abusive or malicious requests. These are operated by our infrastructure provider as part of delivering and securing the Website, not for tracking your browsing for marketing purposes.

5. Functional technologies

The Website does not currently use functional cookies to remember visitor preferences.

6. Analytics

The Website does not currently have analytics enabled. If analytics is introduced in the future, we will conduct a privacy and security assessment first (see our Privacy/Tracking Deployment Gate), update this policy to describe the specific analytics technology and data collected, and apply appropriate consent controls where required.

7. Marketing and advertising

The Website does not use marketing or advertising cookies, and we do not deploy advertising trackers by default. We have no current plans to introduce advertising technology on this Website.

8. Third-party technologies

The Website loads web fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com) to render its typography. Depending on your browser and Google's practices, requests to load these fonts may be visible to Google as the operator of that font service. We do not use Google Fonts for tracking, and no advertising identifiers are set by this integration on our end.

9. Browser controls

Most browsers let you control or clear cookies and site data through their settings. Because this Website does not deploy optional tracking cookies today, there is no cookie-consent banner presented — a banner is not added merely to appear compliant when there is nothing beyond necessary/security-level technology to consent to. If that changes, this policy and the Website's consent controls will be updated accordingly, and, where analytics or marketing technologies are introduced, a consent mechanism reflecting those specific technologies will be added.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy if the technologies actually used on the Website change. Review our Privacy Policy for how we handle personal information more broadly.

11. Contact

Questions about cookies and tracking can be sent to admin@gavriqlabsglobal.com with the subject line PRIVACY: Cookies.
