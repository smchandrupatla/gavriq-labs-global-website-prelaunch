Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 September 2026  ·  Last updated: 7 September 2026

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how GAVRIQ Labs Global ("GAVRIQ Labs Global", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, discloses, secures and retains information in connection with this website and associated website communications (the "Website").

GAVRIQ Labs Global is based in Australia. Our intended future legal entity is GAVRIQ Labs Global Pty Ltd. Corporate registration details (including ABN and ACN) are currently in progress and this policy will be updated once registration is complete. Until then, references to "GAVRIQ Labs Global" describe the business operating this Website.

You can contact us about this policy at admin@gavriqlabsglobal.com.

This policy applies only to this Website and related communications. It does not apply to separate products, client engagements or services, which may be governed by their own agreements or notices.

2. Our privacy principles

We aim to design and operate this Website in line with the following commitments:

  • Data minimisation — we try to collect only what is reasonably necessary for a clear purpose.
  • Transparency — we aim to explain what we collect and why in plain language.
  • Purpose limitation — information is used for the purposes described in this policy, not repurposed without appropriate basis.
  • Appropriate security — we apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards.
  • Restricted access — access to personal information is limited to people who reasonably need it.
  • Reasonable retention — information is not kept longer than reasonably necessary.
  • Access and correction — people may ask to access or correct information we hold about them.
  • Privacy-by-design — privacy considerations are factored into how the Website and its features are built.
  • Responsible AI practices — see Section 6 below.
  • Periodic review — this policy and our internal practices are reviewed periodically.

3. Information that may be collected

Depending on how you interact with the Website, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Organisation or company name
  • Professional information you voluntarily provide (e.g. role, industry)
  • The subject of your enquiry
  • The content of your enquiry or message
  • Records of communications with us
  • Browser and device information (e.g. browser type, general device type)
  • IP address and security-related logs, collected for the purpose of operating and securing the Website
  • Website diagnostic information (e.g. error or performance signals)
  • Analytics information — only if and when analytics is actually enabled on the Website (see Section 7 and our internal processor register)
  • Any other information you choose to voluntarily supply to us

We do not ask for and you should not submit sensitive personal information (such as health, financial account credentials, government identifiers, or similarly sensitive categories of information) through the general enquiry form.

4. How information is collected

We may collect information through:

  • Website forms, such as the contact/enquiry form
  • Direct email correspondence with us
  • Website infrastructure and hosting/security systems used to operate the Website (e.g. request and security logs)
  • Cookies or similar technologies, where applicable — see our Cookie & Tracking Policy
  • Other legitimate business interactions, such as calls or meetings you initiate with us
  • Authorised or publicly available sources where appropriate (for example, publicly listed business contact details)

5. How information is used

We may use information for purposes including:

  • Responding to your enquiry
  • Communicating with you about information or services you have requested
  • Operating, maintaining and improving the Website
  • Maintaining the security of the Website and our systems
  • Detecting, investigating and preventing abuse, fraud or malicious activity
  • Troubleshooting technical issues
  • Improving website reliability and performance
  • Meeting legal, regulatory or compliance obligations
  • Establishing, exercising or defending legal rights
Important: Submitting a website enquiry does not automatically subscribe you to marketing communications. See Section 6 (Marketing consent) below.

6. Marketing consent

We do not currently operate a marketing mailing list through this Website. If marketing functionality is introduced in the future, we commit that marketing consent will be:

  • Separate from submission of a general contact/enquiry form
  • Optional and freely given
  • Unchecked by default (opt-in, not opt-out)
  • Clearly described at the point of collection
  • Recorded, so we can demonstrate when and how consent was given
  • Withdrawable at any time
  • Accompanied by a working unsubscribe mechanism in any marketing communication

A general enquiry will never be treated as marketing consent.

7. Artificial intelligence and personal information

GAVRIQ Labs Global works in artificial intelligence, but working in AI does not mean that information you submit through this Website is automatically used to train AI models.

Website enquiry information is not intentionally used to train public or general-purpose AI models unless doing so is:

  • Clearly disclosed to you in advance,
  • Appropriately authorised,
  • Legally permitted, and
  • Subject to appropriate privacy and security controls.

Any product-specific or client-specific AI processing (for example, in the context of a delivered solution or engagement) may be governed by separate notices, agreements or policies specific to that product or engagement, rather than this Website Privacy Policy.

See our Security & Responsible Disclosure page for how we approach security more broadly, and Section 10 below for how we secure information.

8. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies and similar technologies used on this Website, where applicable, may fall into these categories:

  • Strictly necessary technologies — required for basic website operation.
  • Security technologies — used to help protect the Website and its infrastructure (for example, technologies provided by our content delivery/security provider).
  • Functional technologies — used to remember choices you make while using the Website.
  • Analytics technologies — only if analytics is enabled on the Website.
  • Marketing technologies — only if explicitly introduced in the future, and only with appropriate consent.

We do not claim categories of cookies exist unless they are actually deployed on the Website. For full detail on what is currently in use, see our Cookie & Tracking Policy.

9. Service providers

We may share information with categories of service providers who help us operate the Website and our business, which may include:

  • Hosting, content delivery network (CDN) and security providers
  • Email providers, including a transactional email delivery provider used solely to deliver contact-form enquiry submissions to our own inbox (admin@gavriqlabsglobal.com) — this provider processes the content of your enquiry only for that purpose and does not use it for its own marketing
  • Cloud infrastructure providers
  • Source control and deployment tooling providers
  • Analytics providers, only if analytics is enabled
  • Professional services providers (e.g. legal, accounting), where necessary

We do not claim a specific processing arrangement exists unless it is reflected in our internal processor register, which we maintain and review (see Section 16 of this Website's Phase 1 implementation for governance detail).

10. Overseas processing

Some technology and service providers we use may process information outside Australia, for example because a provider operates global infrastructure. We do not make specific claims about exact countries beyond what our internal processor register confirms. We take reasonable steps to only work with providers who apply appropriate security and privacy safeguards.

11. Security of personal information

We aim to apply reasonable measures to protect information, including:

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS)
  • Access controls and the principle of least privilege
  • Secure development practices
  • Vulnerability management
  • Logging of security-relevant events
  • Infrastructure security controls provided by our hosting/CDN/security provider
  • Change control over Website updates and deployments
  • An incident response process — see Section 15
  • Retention and deletion practices appropriate to the type of information

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If you become aware of a security issue affecting this Website, please see our Security & Responsible Disclosure page.

12. Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for enquiry handling, legitimate business purposes, security, dispute resolution, or legal/regulatory obligations. As an internal governance baseline (not a statutory requirement unless separately noted), we generally aim to apply the following:

CategoryIndicative retention
Ordinary website enquiriesApproximately 24 months after the last meaningful interaction
Unsuccessful sales enquiriesApproximately 24 months
Website and security logsApproximately 30–90 days, unless required for an active investigation
Security vulnerability reportsApproximately 5 years after closure
Marketing consent evidence (if applicable)While subscribed, plus a reasonable evidence period
Unsubscribe / suppression records (if applicable)Retained as necessary to honour opt-outs
