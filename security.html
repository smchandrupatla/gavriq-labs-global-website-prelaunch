Security & Responsible Disclosure

Effective date: 7 September 2026

1. Our approach

GAVRIQ Labs Global takes the security of this Website seriously and welcomes reports from good-faith security researchers. This page explains how to report a suspected vulnerability and what we expect from responsible research.

2. How to report a vulnerability

Email admin@gavriqlabsglobal.com with the subject line SECURITY: Vulnerability Report. Please include, where possible:

  • The affected URL or system
  • A description of the vulnerability
  • Steps to reproduce it
  • The expected security impact
  • Supporting evidence (e.g. screenshots, request/response samples)
  • Contact information so we can follow up

Machine-readable contact details are also published at /.well-known/security.txt in line with RFC 9116.

3. Responsible research expectations

We ask researchers to act in good faith and, in particular, to:

  • Avoid accessing, modifying or deleting data that is not your own
  • Avoid actions that disrupt or degrade the Website or its availability
  • Avoid denial-of-service testing of any kind
  • Avoid social engineering of our staff, contractors or users
  • Avoid physical-security testing or attacks
  • Avoid the use of malware
  • Minimise privacy impact, and stop testing immediately if you encounter sensitive or personal information that is not your own — please report it to us instead of continuing
  • Give us reasonable time to investigate and remediate a confirmed issue before any public disclosure

Testing conducted in line with this page is authorised for the purpose of responsible disclosure to us. We do not currently offer a paid bug bounty program; we will acknowledge and, where appropriate, credit good-faith reports, but no financial reward is promised unless we introduce an actual bounty program in future.

4. What happens after you report

We aim to acknowledge reports in a reasonable timeframe, investigate promptly, and keep you informed of progress towards remediation. Response times can vary depending on complexity and severity.

5. Scope

This policy covers the public Website at gavriqlabsglobal.com and its associated infrastructure that we directly control. It does not extend to third-party services we merely link to.

6. Related pages

See our Privacy Policy for how we handle personal information, and our Terms of Use for the rules governing use of the Website, including security research boundaries.

7. Contact

Email: admin@gavriqlabsglobal.com — subject SECURITY: Vulnerability Report.
