WHAT WE DO

Two things we do deeply.

We lead with the work we would stake the company on, and stay honest about the rest.

01

Agents for regulated back-office work

Agents that close the loop on document-heavy process work — intake, triage, KYC review, reconciliation — with a human approval step and a full decision trail on every action.

02

Evaluation & AI governance

The layer most AI projects skip: offline and online evals, observability, red-teaming, safety controls and the audit evidence your risk and compliance teams will ask for.

We also build the supporting pieces these systems need — retrieval and knowledge platforms, document and multimodal pipelines, and the cloud-native APIs and data services underneath. Ask us about a specific stack.

HOW WE WORK

Reason. Build. Improve.

Three stages, each with a defined output — so you always know what you are getting, and when.

01 · Reason
[1–2 weeks]

Decide whether AI is the right tool

We map the process, find where judgement actually happens, and define what “good enough to act on” means in measurable terms.

Output — scoped problem statement, success threshold, and an honest go / no-go.
02 · Build
[4–8 weeks]

Ship a system into real conditions

Production engineering from day one — real data, real integrations, and human approval wherever the stakes require it.

Output — a working system, its eval harness, and the runbook to operate it.
03 · Improve
[ongoing]

Keep it accurate as reality moves

Monitoring, regression evals on every change, drift review and a governance record that holds up to scrutiny.

Output — a quality loop your team can run without us.
